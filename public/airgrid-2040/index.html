Explore assumptions ↓
What is this model actually calculating?

This is a conceptual interactive exhibit, not a traffic forecast or transport feasibility assessment. The map is schematic. Station coordinates approximate real areas. Distances are straight-line estimates. Flight time assumes 150 km/h average horizontal speed plus 7 minutes for access and handling. The road scenario assumes a route 1.48 times the straight-line distance, 27 km/h average road speed, plus 7 minutes. The fleet/wait curve is an invented illustrative relationship, not a measured operational model. Real performance depends on regulation, weather, noise, safety, vertiport capacity, airspace separation, energy, cost and demand.
